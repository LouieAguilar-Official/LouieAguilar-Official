## 📡 GPR & Ground Penetrating Radar Skills

- **Field Operations:** Operating GPR equipment for subsurface investigation and underground target detection.
- **Survey Preparation:** Preparing survey areas, establishing survey lines, and configuring acquisition parameters according to site conditions and project objectives.
- **Equipment Configuration:** Working with antenna frequencies, time windows, sampling parameters, trace intervals, stacking, and encoder settings.
- **Electromagnetic Fundamentals:** Understanding relative dielectric permittivity (RDP), electrical conductivity, wave velocity, propagation, and attenuation.
- **Data Acquisition:** Collecting radar profiles and evaluating signal quality, reflections, noise, and subsurface responses.
- **Radargram Processing:** Applying dewow, background removal, DC removal, stacking, gain adjustments, and bandpass filtering.
- **Radargram Interpretation:** Evaluating hyperbolic diffraction patterns, reflection events, signal polarity, ringing, and potential subsurface anomalies.
- **Velocity & Depth Analysis:** Using two-way travel time and estimated electromagnetic wave velocity to calculate potential target depths.
- **Underground Investigation:** Surveying for potential buried utilities, pipes, conduits, and other subsurface features.
- **Technical Reporting:** Organizing survey observations, processed data, interpretations, and results for project documentation.
